# Choose a signal plan

Go to **Apps → Signal Plan**.

A signal plan is a **subscription with a fixed term**. It is what grants access to the signal feed at a given tier. Without an active plan the engine's live feed is not available to your account.

**Starter** — for individuals getting started with automated signals.
**Growth** — for active traders scaling their portfolio performance. Marked *Most Popular*.
**Elite** — for professionals and teams running high-frequency strategies.

## Monthly or annual

Annual is charged **once for the whole year** at a discounted monthly rate — roughly 20% off across every tier.

| Tier | Monthly × 12 | Annual price | You save |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | $348 | $276 | $72 (~21%) |
| Growth | $948 | $756 | $192 (~20%) |
| Elite | $2,388 | $1,908 | $480 (~20%) |

{% hint style="info" %}
Annual is billed as a **single 365-day term**, not as a cheaper recurring subscription. Nothing renews automatically — when the term ends, the entitlement lapses and you buy again. That is a deliberate design choice, not an oversight: there is no stored payment method to charge.
{% endhint %}

## Term lengths

| Period | Term |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly | 30 days |
| Annual | 365 days |

The term starts at purchase, not at the start of a calendar month.

## How to buy

1. Open **Signal Plan** and choose a tier and period.
2. Pay from your **account balance**, or start a **crypto checkout** if the balance is short. See [Paying with crypto](../billing/paying-with-crypto.md).
3. The entitlement activates immediately on settlement.

{% hint style="success" %}
The price is looked up **server-side from the item id**. Your browser sends an identifier, never an amount — so a modified request cannot name its own price.
{% endhint %}

## Buying a plan you already hold

You cannot. If the same subscription is already active, the purchase is refused with *"That subscription is already active."* rather than silently taking the money and overwriting the term.

Wait for the term to lapse, or buy a different tier.

## What happens at expiry

The entitlement stops being granted. Entitlements are evaluated **per request** against the expiry timestamp rather than cached in your session, so expiry takes effect immediately rather than at your next sign-in.

Nothing else is affected: your balance, deposits, referral earnings and verification status are unchanged.
